A little more clarity

About the LearningIQ approach

How LearningIQ connects skills, learning and career development.

Does LearningIQ guarantee a job?

No. It can help connect validated skills to relevant opportunities and prepare an application handoff. Applications, selection and hiring outcomes remain with the relevant external services or employers.

Does completing a course automatically earn a certificate?

No. Course completion, assessment results, validation findings and issued credentials are distinct. Certification depends on the assessment requirements and the issuer’s decision, with verification supported where available.

How does the learning journey work?

LearningIQ is designed to connect learner profiling, skills assessment, skill gaps, personalized learning, practical application, competency validation, credentials, opportunity access, ongoing growth and outcome measurement.

Is LearningIQ just another course platform?

No. The proposed platform connects learner profiles, skill assessment, gap analysis, personalized learning, practice, competency validation, credentials, opportunities and measurable outcomes.

How is progress different from competency?

Finishing an activity records progress. Verified competency requires suitable assessment evidence and a validation decision. The model keeps these records separate.

How does the platform personalize learning?

It uses a learner's goals, current skills, evidence, learning activity and gaps relative to a chosen target. The proposed intelligence framework can use those inputs to recommend a path and refine it as evidence changes.
